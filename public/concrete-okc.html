The Problem

Why Most OKC Concrete Fails Within 5 Years

Oklahoma City sits on some of the most difficult soil in the country. Our expansive clay shifts dramatically between wet and dry seasons, creating underground movement that destroys concrete from below.

Most contractors skip the prep work that prevents this. They pour directly on unstable ground, use thin slabs without reinforcement, and cut control joints in the wrong places. The result is predictable — cracking, settling, and heaving within a few years.

Add in OKC's freeze-thaw cycles, 100°F summer days that accelerate curing too fast, and heavy rain that erodes unprotected edges — and cheap concrete becomes expensive concrete very quickly.

Our Approach

Concrete Built for Oklahoma Conditions

Every project starts with the ground, not the concrete. We excavate to proper depth, compact the subgrade, and install a gravel base that absorbs soil movement and drains water away from the slab.

We use 3,000–4,000 PSI air-entrained concrete that resists freeze-thaw damage and Oklahoma's temperature extremes. Steel reinforcement and fiber mesh are standard — not upgrades. Control joints are placed based on engineering, not guesswork.

The finish matters too. We cure slowly and evenly to prevent surface cracking, apply sealant to protect against moisture penetration, and ensure proper drainage slope on every pour. It takes longer. It costs a little more. And it lasts decades instead of years.

Process

How We Work

From first call to final walkthrough — clear communication at every step

01

Site Assessment

We evaluate soil conditions, drainage, existing concrete, and access. You get an honest assessment and detailed written estimate.

02

Preparation

Old concrete removed and hauled away. Subgrade excavated, compacted, and gravel base installed. Forms set to exact grade.

03

Reinforcement

Steel rebar grid or fiber mesh installed based on load requirements. Expansion joints and control joints planned for the slab geometry.

04

Pour & Finish

Concrete poured, vibrated, screeded, and finished to your chosen texture. We manage curing speed for Oklahoma's temperature conditions.

05

Cure & Seal

Proper curing compound applied. Sealant protects against moisture, staining, and UV damage. Edges finished and forms removed.

06

Final Walkthrough

We walk the project together, review the work, and discuss maintenance. Complete cleanup — your property left cleaner than we found it.
